    The engine inside

    Aria2 Next

    Redefining the next generation of aria2

    Aria2 Next is the open-source engine that powers Rayburst: aria2, actively maintained, with a long backlog of upstream bugs fixed and ED2K support added for the first time. HTTP, HTTPS, SFTP, Metalink, ED2K and BitTorrent, from the command line or over JSON-RPC.

    Native HLS and DASH as well: choose tracks, record live broadcasts, and save to MP4 or MKV without re-encoding.
